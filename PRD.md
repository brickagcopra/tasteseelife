## 1. Executive Summary

Taste & See is a **subscription-based hospitality wellness ecosystem** that connects aging adults and their families with professionally trained chefs, culinary companions, caregivers, and concierge lifestyle providers. The platform is positioned not as a clinical caregiving app, but as a **hospitality-driven lifestyle and wellness companion platform** built around the philosophy of **Trust + Human Warmth + Culinary Expertise** and the emotional concept of **Food + Memory + Companionship**.

The platform operates as a **three-sided marketplace** combining elements of Airbnb (trusted in-home services), Care.com (vetted providers), Uber (on-demand booking and dispatch), ClassPass (recurring memberships), and luxury concierge (white-glove service). Revenue derives from family/senior subscriptions, chef/caregiver subscriptions, marketplace booking commissions (10–30%), academy certification fees, and enterprise partnerships with senior residences, healthcare organizations, and corporate caregiver benefit programs.

Initial launch targets Manhattan's Upper East Side, with phased expansion across NYC and national rollout into luxury aging, wellness, and concierge markets.

To become **America's leading culinary companionship and aging-in-place wellness platform** — the trusted name when families need a meaningful, nourishing presence in a loved one's life.

## 2. Problem Statement

Aging adults and their families face a constellation of overlapping challenges that no single existing service adequately addresses:

- **Loneliness and isolation** — Roughly one in three older adults experience persistent loneliness, with measurable impacts on mortality and cognitive decline.
- **Meal fatigue** — Repetitive, microwaved, or institutional meals lead to undernourishment and loss of joy in eating.
- **Caregiver burnout** — Adult children juggling careers, parenting, and elder care reach the limits of bandwidth and expertise.
- **Transportation limitations** — Loss of driving privileges severs seniors from social, cultural, and medical engagement.
- **Loss of routine and community** — Retirement, widowhood, and reduced mobility erode daily structure.
- **Cognitive decline** — Dementia and mild cognitive impairment require sensitive, patient interaction not found in food-delivery or rideshare apps.
- **Emotional disconnection** — Existing senior-care apps feel medical, transactional, and undignified.

Existing alternatives fail in distinct ways: meal kits skip the human connection; home health agencies feel clinical and shift-based; private chefs are unaffordable and unstructured; companion-care apps are not curated for hospitality or culinary excellence. **Taste & See addresses all of these in a single integrated, trust-first platform.**

## 3. Target Users & Personas

### 3.1 Primary Personas

#### Persona A — "The Adult Child Buyer" (Decision Maker, Payer)

- **Age:** 45–65
- **Income:** $200K+ household
- **Location:** NYC, Boston, SF, LA, Miami, Chicago
- **Pain:** Lives in a different city than aging parent; guilt, anxiety, time poverty
- **Goal:** Peace of mind that mom/dad is fed, engaged, and emotionally cared for
- **Trigger:** Recent fall, hospitalization, dementia diagnosis, or holiday visit revealing decline

#### Persona B — "The Aging Parent" (End User of Service)

- **Age:** 70–95
- **Living situation:** Aging in place — own home, condo, or luxury senior residence
- **Pain:** Loneliness, meal monotony, loss of routine, reluctance to admit need for help
- **Goal:** Dignity, autonomy, joy, connection, comfort food tied to identity and memory
- **Tech literacy:** Variable — interface must be navigable by family on their behalf

#### Persona C — "The Culinary Companion Provider"

- **Background:** Trained chef, hospitality professional, certified caregiver, or culinary student
- **Income goal:** $40–$120/hr depending on tier
- **Motivation:** Meaningful work, flexible schedule, professional credibility, recurring clients
- **Pain in current market:** Low-trust gig platforms, no certification pathway, no client retention

#### Persona D — "The Partner Facility Administrator"

- **Role:** Activities Director / Wellness Coordinator at luxury senior residence, adult day care, or hospital
- **Goal:** Differentiated amenity for residents, retention of high-paying tenants, post-discharge support
- **Procurement:** Annual contracts, RFPs, pilot programs

### 3.2 Secondary Personas

- **Corporate HR Benefits Director** — purchasing caregiver benefits for employees
- **Discharge Planner / Social Worker** — referring post-hospitalization clients
- **MLTC Care Manager** — coordinating supplemental in-home services

### 10.1 Dashboard & Analytics

- Cohort retention analysis
- Geographic heatmaps
- Funnel analytics (signup → trial → paid → retention)

### 10.2 User Management

- Search across families, seniors, providers, partners, students, and admins
- Account detail view with full activity history
- Impersonation (audit-logged) for support
- Suspension, ban, and re-instatement workflows
- KYC document review queue
- Background check status

### 10.3 Subscription Management

- Affiliate / referral codes

### 10.5 Booking & Marketplace Management

### 10.6 Concierge Operations

### 10.7 Provider Operations

### 10.8 Accounting System

A dedicated double-entry accounting subsystem with the following capabilities:

- **Chart of accounts** (assets, liabilities, equity, revenue, expense) with sub-accounts per business line (subscription revenue per tier, marketplace commission revenue, academy revenue, partnership revenue, refunds, payment processing fees, payouts payable, deferred revenue, taxes payable, etc.)
- **Journal entries** automatically generated from platform events:
  - New subscription → DR Cash / CR Deferred Revenue
  - Subscription period elapses → DR Deferred Revenue / CR Subscription Revenue
  - Booking completed → DR Cash / CR Marketplace Revenue + CR Provider Payable
  - Provider payout → DR Provider Payable / CR Cash
  - Refund → reverse appropriate entries
  - Coupon discount → contra-revenue entry
- **General ledger** with drill-down
- **Trial balance, balance sheet, income statement, cash flow statement**
- **SaaS metrics**: MRR, ARR, ARPU, LTV, CAC, gross margin, net revenue retention, gross revenue retention
- **Provider payouts ledger** with reconciliation against Stripe Connect
- **Tax reporting**: sales tax accrual where applicable, 1099 generation
- **Multi-currency readiness** (national expansion)
- **Audit trail** — every entry immutable, reversal entries only
- **Period close** workflows
- **Export to QuickBooks / NetSuite** for finance team
- **Reconciliation** between platform records and Stripe / bank statements

## 11. Non-Functional Requirements

### 11.1 Performance

- P95 API latency < 250ms for read-heavy endpoints
- P95 search latency < 500ms
- App cold start < 2.5s on mid-tier devices
- 99.95% uptime for booking and payment paths

### 11.2 Scalability

- Designed to support 100K active senior households and 25K active providers nationally without re-architecture
- Booking and messaging must scale horizontally
- Activity log and message storage must handle high write volume (Cassandra)

### 11.3 Security

- SOC 2 Type II readiness from day one
- HIPAA-aligned for healthcare partner workflows (BAA-ready architecture)
- PCI DSS compliance via Stripe (no card data on platform)
- Encryption at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3)
- Hardware-backed key management (KMS / HSM)
- Field-level encryption for sensitive PII
- Background-check data segregation
- Penetration testing annually

### 11.4 Privacy & Compliance

- CCPA / state privacy law compliance
- Data subject access and deletion workflows
- Senior/family consent management for photos and recordings
- HIPAA-aligned handling of any health-flagged data
- Mandated reporter compliance for elder abuse
- Robust audit trail

### 11.5 Accessibility

- WCAG 2.2 AA across web and mobile
- Senior-mode UI: large type, high contrast, simplified navigation
- Screen reader support, keyboard navigation
- Multilingual support (English, Spanish, Mandarin in Phase 2)

### 11.6 Reliability

- Automated failover for critical services
- Database backups with 30-day retention and point-in-time recovery
- Disaster recovery RPO < 15 min, RTO < 1 hr

### 11.7 Observability

- Structured logging across all services
- Distributed tracing
- Real-user monitoring on web and mobile
- Synthetic checks for booking, payment, search

### 11.8 Localization

- Initial: en-US
- Phase 2: es-US, zh-CN
- Currency: USD only initially
- Time-zone awareness for booking accuracy

## 16. Glossary

- **Companion Dining** — A booked experience where a chef or culinary companion prepares and shares a meal with the senior, providing both nourishment and company.
- **Memory Meal** — A culturally or biographically meaningful dish prepared to evoke positive memory and emotional comfort.
- **Concierge Lifestyle** — Tier 3 service tier featuring dedicated, white-glove ongoing engagement.
- **Provider** — Any chef, culinary companion, caregiver, or hospitality professional active on the platform.
- **Partner** — An institutional client (residence, hospital, employer, healthcare org).
- **GMV** — Gross Merchandise Value (sum of all booking transactions).
- **MRR / ARR** — Monthly / Annual Recurring Revenue.
- **MLTC** — Managed Long-Term Care.
